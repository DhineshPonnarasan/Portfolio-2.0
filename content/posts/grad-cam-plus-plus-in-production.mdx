A short note on swapping Grad-CAM for Grad-CAM++ in a clinically-assisted brain-tumour MRI classifier.

## What changed

- Vanilla Grad-CAM highlighted large regions of healthy tissue.
- Grad-CAM++ localised the tumour mask more cleanly on ResNet-50 features.
- Radiologist trust in the saliency map went up noticeably in the second review cycle.

## What we'd still improve

The saliency map is still a 2D heatmap. Clinicians asked for a 3D voxel view, which is on the roadmap.
