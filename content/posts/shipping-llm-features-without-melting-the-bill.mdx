A short field note on shipping LLM-backed features without surprising the finance team.

## The core idea

Cache aggressively, stream early, and never trust an upstream's status page. Every request that costs money should also generate a structured log line.

## Three cheap wins

1. **Bounded body size** — a 32 KB cap catches accidental pastes of full PDFs.
2. **Layered rate limits** — per-IP per-minute *and* per-hour, both kept in memory.
3. **Streaming with heartbeats** — clients see the first token in under 800 ms even on a cold cache.

That's the surface area. Everything else is monitoring.
